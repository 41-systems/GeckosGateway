Network Safety & Port Testing

Check how your own internet connection or authorized systems appear from the public internet. These tests are performed by the external services below; Gecko's Gateway does not run a port scan itself.

🛡️ GRC ShieldsUP! ↗ 🔌 YouGetSignal Port Checker ↗ 🔎 Port Checker ↗

What the results mean

Open means a service accepted an inbound connection. Closed means the host responded but no service accepted the connection. Stealth/filtered generally means probes were dropped or no response was received.

Use only on systems you own or have permission to test. For your home connection, these tools are useful for checking router/firewall exposure and unexpected internet-facing services.
